Famous Temples in Indore

Khajrana Ganesh Temple

Citizens of Indore city and other nearby cities have great faith in the Khajrana temple. This temple was made by Ahilyabai Holkar, the brave Maratha queen. This is an important place of Hindus. Khajrana Ganesha Temple was constructed by Rani Ahilyabai Holkar. This temple is one of the famous Hindu temples in India. Mostly wednesday and sunday huge people will come to this temple for offering prayers. As per a local belief, all the wishes of the devotees are fulfilled, once they worship in this temple. The main festival of this temple is vinayak chathurthi and was held in grand manner in the month of August and september The temple is taken over by the government. The temple is managed by the Bhatt family. It is believed that in order to safe guard the idol from Aurangzeb, the idol was hidden in a well and in 1735, it was taken out from the well and a temple was established in 1735 by Ahilyabai Holkar belonging to the Holkar dynasty of the Maratha Empire. .

Ranjeet Hanuman

Shri Ranjeet Hanuman Mandir is located on Footi Kothi Road in Indore,Madhya Pradesh. This is the only Hanuman temple in the world where Hanuman Ji is standing with a shield and sword in his hand and Ahiravana at his feet. The temple was established 125 years ago. It is believed that many kings used to come here to seek the blessings of victory before fighting a war and to date, no one has been defeated after taking blessings. Festivals like Hanuman Jayanti are celebrated with great enthusiasm.

Annapurna Temple

he Annapurna Temple is located in the heart of Indore, a bustling city in the Indian state of Madhya Pradesh. Known for its rich history, vibrant culture, and architectural grandeur, Indore is a city that beautifully blends tradition with modernity. The Annapurna Temple, dedicated to the goddess of food, Annapurna Devi, is a significant religious site. The temple, adorned with intricate carvings and a magnificent architectural style, is famous for its four life-sized elephants that guard the entrance. It's believed that the temple was built to honour the goddess who ensures the nourishment of her children.

Pitra parvat

Pitreshwar hanuman temple , which is situated in Pitra Parvat, Indore, is a Bhavya Hanuman temple which is famous for its 72 feet high idol and interesting light and sound show. Its distance from Indore is 12 kilometers and there is a huge crowd of devotees here every day. This temple is a spiritual and interesting place which is a must visit for every Hanuman devotee! Indore, which is an important city of Madhya Pradesh, is known for its religious and spiritual diversity. There are many famous temples here which hold special significance for the devotees. One of them is Pitreshwar Hanuman Mandir, which is famous for its divine idol and interesting history. This temple is situated on Pitra Parvat which is one of the most elevated places in Indore. The specialty of this temple is that a 72 feet tall statue of Hanuman ji is installed here which gives a wonderful experience to the devotees.
