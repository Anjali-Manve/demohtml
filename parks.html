Famous Parks in Indore

Meghdoot Garden

Meghdoot Garden is one of the largest and most beautiful gardens in Indore. It is known for its lush greenery, fountains, jogging tracks, and serene environment. A perfect place for family outings, morning walks, and photography lovers.

Nehru Park

Nehru Park is a popular destination for families and kids in Indore. It features a zoo, toy train, swimming pool, and a mini amusement park. The green surroundings make it a refreshing getaway in the heart of the city.

Pipliyapala Regional Park

Pipliyapala Regional Park is a beautifully developed garden offering boat rides, musical fountains, and landscaped gardens. It is perfect for evening strolls, boating, and enjoying natural beauty with family and friends.

Tafreeh Agro Park

Tafreeh Agro Park is an adventure-cum-nature park on the outskirts of Indore. It offers activities like zip-lining, rope climbing, and eco-friendly rides. It is a great spot for a one-day picnic full of fun and adventure.
