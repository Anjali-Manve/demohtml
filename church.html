Famous Churches in Indore

White Church

Constructed in 1858, the White Church of Indore is one of the most prominent landmarks in the city. This colonial-era church was built during British rule and is known for its classic white façade, well-maintained gardens, and calm spiritual atmosphere. It continues to attract both worshippers and tourists.

St. Francis of Assisi Cathedral

St. Francis Cathedral is another major church in Indore, known for its serene setting and regular masses. The cathedral is dedicated to Saint Francis of Assisi and features Roman Catholic architecture, a tall bell tower, and a peaceful prayer hall — making it a spiritual escape within the city.
