Famous Stadiums in Indore

Holkar Cricket Stadium

Holkar Stadium is one of the most iconic cricket stadiums in India, located in Indore. It is known for hosting national and international cricket matches. The stadium offers a vibrant atmosphere and has witnessed several historic cricketing moments. It is the home ground for the Madhya Pradesh cricket team.

Nehru Stadium

Nehru Stadium, situated in the heart of Indore, is a multi-purpose stadium that has hosted cricket, football, and athletic events. Built in 1964, it has a seating capacity of around 25,000 spectators. The stadium has a historical significance for older cricket tournaments and regional games.
