Famous Markets in Indore

Rajwada Market

Rajwada Market, located near the historic Rajwada Palace, is famous for its vibrant bazaars offering ethnic clothes, jewelry, handicrafts, and traditional items. It reflects the colorful and royal heritage of Indore and is a must-visit for shoppers and history lovers.

Ranipura Market

Ranipura Market is well known for its textile and garment shops. It is a paradise for those looking for affordable clothing, dress materials, and wholesale fabrics. The buzzing streets are filled with colorful shops and lively bargaining scenes.

MT Cloth Market

MT Cloth Market is the largest wholesale cloth market of Indore. From luxurious sarees to trendy dress materials, it offers a huge variety at wholesale rates. It is popular among local retailers and visitors seeking bulk shopping.

Topkhana Market

Topkhana Market is a traditional street market famous for budget shopping. You will find everything here — clothes, accessories, household items, and more at pocket-friendly prices. It's a true reflection of Indore’s local shopping culture.
